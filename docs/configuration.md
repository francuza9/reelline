# Configuration reference

Generated by `reelline config docs` from the schema (`engine/src/reelline/config/schema/`); do not edit by hand.
A channel's `config.yaml` sets what differs from these defaults. Paths are relative to the channel directory.
Credentials are never in the config: a field marked *(env variable name)* names the variable in the channel's `.env` that holds the value. Long defaults are cut with `…`; `reelline config show` prints the merged config in full. Any setting can also be overridden for one run with `--set section.key=value`.

### `models`

Every model the engine calls, one role per use (reelline/providers/registry.py get_llm(role)):
writer       the script's story pass, its retry, the trial, the repair rounds (reelline/pipeline/script.py)
stylist      the punch-up pass (and the flirt line's)
proofreader  the proofread of the script and of the caption's hook
caption      the caption (hook, title, hashtags)
endcard      the end card's lines
assessor     the pick's story assessment (reelline/pipeline/assess.py, techpick.py)
briefer      the news brief (reelline/pipeline/brief.py)
checker      the fact-check of the script, its fx graphics and the end card (factcheck.py)
vision       the image picker's check of a candidate (reelline/media/images/vision.py)
qa_vision    QA's look at the rendered frames (reelline/pipeline/qa.py)
illustrator  the generated fallback image (reelline/media/images/sources.py)
tts          the voice (reelline/media/tts.py, scripts/render_reel.py)

| Key | Type | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `prices` | dict[str, dict[str, float]] | per channel | USD per 1M tokens: input, output (thinking is billed as output), output_image (image models). Every role's model and refusal fallback must be listed (reelline/providers/costs.py). |

### `steps`

One section per pipeline step: its settings, enabled and use (StepConf). A name not listed
here is a channel's own step (CustomStep, with its use: path). script, endcard, produce,
run_daily, retention and analytics are settings of the steps that read them (write and style,
cards, render, tick, rank, stats), not steps of their own.
